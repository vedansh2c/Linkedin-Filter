# Privacy

LinkedIn Word Filter reads the visible text of posts on the LinkedIn feed to decide which posts to hide. It stores only the words or phrases you enter and the enabled setting in Firefox's local extension storage. It does not collect, transmit, or share your data. There are no analytics, remote services, tracking pixels, or network requests in the extension.

The extension requests access only to `https://www.linkedin.com/*` to run its filter, plus Firefox's `storage` permission to remember your settings. It is disabled in private browsing. Turning filtering off or uninstalling the extension restores LinkedIn's normal feed display. Uninstalling removes the extension's stored settings.

LinkedIn itself continues to operate under its own privacy practices. This extension is independent of LinkedIn.
